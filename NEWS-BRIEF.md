## The Hague — ICJ Myanmar verdict

**Headline:** MYANMAR GUILTY OF GENOCIDE
**Date:** 19 November 2026 (The Hague)
**Story:** The International Court of Justice finds Myanmar committed genocide
against the Rohingya minority in 2017 — the first time in the court's
80-year history a state has been held responsible for genocide. 9–6 vote;
Myanmar ordered to pay reparations and allow safe return home.
**Sources:** CNN, 19 Nov 2026; Le Monde, 20 Nov 2026; Reuters, 19 Nov 2026.

## Riyadh — PIF Tower strike

**Headline:** MISSILE HITS RIYADH TOWER
**Date:** 19 November 2026 (Riyadh, Saudi Arabia)
**Story:** A suspected Houthi missile damages the 80-storey PIF Tower,
headquarters of Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund, in the King Abdullah
Financial District — windows blown out across 7–8 floors, metal facade bent,
no casualties reported. Second strike on the district in under two weeks.
**Sources:** Reuters, 19 Nov 2026; AFP via press reports, 19 Nov 2026.

## Odisha — Cyclone Akvan
