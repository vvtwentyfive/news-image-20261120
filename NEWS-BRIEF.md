### 3. UN court finds Myanmar guilty of genocide against the Rohingya
- **Date:** 19 Nov 2026

### 4. COP31 enters closing day with fossil fuels erased from draft deal
- **Date:** 20 Nov 2026
- **Summary:** COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye, entered its scheduled closing day on Friday with no final deal, after a slimmed-down draft published on Thursday — cut from 144 paragraphs to 80 — removed every reference to fossil fuels and dropped a proposed $120 billion-a-year adaptation finance figure.
